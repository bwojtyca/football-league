## The ten most important problems

1. **Player names follow no single rule.** Where: awkward-01 (Elo: "Krzysztof"), awkward-03 (potato: "Krzysztof-Maksymilian Brz…"), awkward-06, awkward-10 (table "Piotr", summary "Piotr Wiśniewski"), awkward-01 "Mniej niż 5 meczów: Jędrzej, Natalia Nowak, Wiktor". Why: the same person looks different from view to view, the ranking would be ambiguous with two Annas, and full names are what get cut. Fix: one helper (e.g. `shortName(player, league)`) that returns the shortest unique form within the league (first name; first name + surname initial when first names collide; "Anna M." for "Anna Maria" only if needed), used in every compact place (rankings in all four views, tables, lists, cards, summary lines, rails); full name only in the profile header, the players list and tooltips.
2. **Navigation is split across four places, and settings hide in the league switcher.** Where: 22-app-menu (≡: Język, Którym graczem jesteś?, Ranking ogólny), 23-league-switcher and awkward-13 (title ▾: Zaproś, Ustawienia ligi, Zmień ligę, Ligi), the desktop sidebar, cards on the pages; ≡ also on 4-desktop/* and 3-tablet/* next to the sidebar. Why: a user looking for settings or the language has to guess which of two top-bar menus holds it; duplicated paths make the model fuzzy. Fix: one league hub behind the title (league actions + "Ligi" + "Ranking ogólny" + "Którym graczem jesteś?" + language at the bottom) and remove ≡; on ≥1000 px the sidebar already lists these, so add only "Język" to its footer.
3. **Names on the game table are rotated and, on small phones, cut.** Where: 2-phone/27, 1-phone-small/27, 1-phone-small/awkward-14 ("Krzys…", "Grzeg…", "Małg…"), 3-tablet/27. Why: the game screen is read at a glance while standing at the table; vertical text and cut names slow the one decision that matters (whose rod scored). Fix: put the name horizontally in a plate at each handle pair (the boards above/below already show full first names: reuse the short unique name there), or show jersey number + initials on the rail and keep the full names only on the boards; never cut below the short name.
4. **Surnames cut by ellipsis in lists and headers.** Where: 2-phone/awkward-06 (2), awkward-03 (6, 7 at 360 px), awkward-08 (partners/rivals), awkward-09 ("Krzysztof-Ma…"), awkward-12 ("Brzęczyszczyki.."), awkward-01 tournament card. Why: truncated identity text; the hero even uses two dots instead of an ellipsis. Fix: allow two lines (`-webkit-line-clamp: 2`) for names in list rows and the hero, use problem 1's short name in narrow columns (partners, compare header, potato), and give a `title` with the full name.
5. **On phones the ranking starts below three cards.** Where: 2-phone/01-03 (ranking starts at y≈405 of 915), 1-phone-small/01 (2.5 rows visible), awkward-01. Why: "see where they stand" is the app's second job; the who-are-you prompt, the live game and the tournament card all have the same weight as the ranking above it. Fix: after "you" is chosen collapse the prompt (already done: the you card); before that, make "Którym graczem jesteś?" a one-line chip; merge the live game and the tournament card into one compact "Teraz" strip (one row each, 56 px), so the first 5 ranking rows fit on a 640 px phone.
6. **Desktop games list zig-zags.** Where: 4-desktop/04, 4-desktop/awkward-04, 5-wide/04. Why: day headings label only the left column; the right column's games float without a heading, odd days leave holes and separators misalign when one side wraps the tournament name. Fix: make each day a grid section whose heading spans both columns (`grid-column: 1 / -1`) with that day's games flowing beneath it in two columns; or keep one column and use the width for meta (time, rules, tournament, Elo change) on the right of each row.
7. **The game screen's secondary controls are unclear and equal in weight.** Where: 2-phone/27, 1-phone-small/27, 4-desktop/27, awkward-14. Why: two icon-only buttons (red/blue arrows) under "Zamień" do not say they swap a team's defence and attack; Cofnij, Samobój… and the swaps are four identical dark pills, so the frequent undo does not stand out. Fix: label the swaps "Zamień czerwonych" / "Zamień niebieskich" (or put a swap icon on each team's board/rail), keep Cofnij as the most prominent of the bottom controls (tonal with the last-event text inside it), move "Samobój…" to a text button.
8. **Win/loss dots and Elo change have no legend.** Where: 4-desktop/01 and 3-tablet/01 ("Ostatnie 5"), 2-phone/11 and 15 ("Ostatnie mecze: ●●○○●"), 2-phone/01 ("−13" under 1538). Why: hollow vs filled carries the meaning with nothing to decode it; a newcomer cannot tell which is a win, and "−13" under the leader can read as a gap.
