## Group 1: league ranking views (01 Elo, 02 Duets, 03 Potato; awkward-01..03; all sizes)

Files: */01-league-ranking.png, */02-league-duets.png, _/03-league-potato.png, {1-phone-small,2-phone,4-desktop}/awkward-0[1-3]-_.png

Good: one clear structure (identity card / "Teraz w lidze" / view switch / list); the leader's place in ball yellow; shared places (5, 5, 7) are right; Elo deltas coloured with enough contrast; players under the threshold listed apart ("Mniej niż 5 meczów: ..."); the "me" row is filled and tagged "Ty"; desktop and wide put the ranking beside the "now" column.

Problems:

- (Readability, Minor) The "+1"/"+3" title-count pill next to the name (2-phone/01: Ewa "+1", Ala "+3") uses the same "+N" form as the Elo change printed on the same row ("-13", "+12"). On the highlighted "me" row the pill outline (#232623 on #202328, ~1:1) vanishes, so "+3" reads as a bare plus number (2-phone/awkward-01 row 3, 1-phone-small/awkward-01). A newcomer cannot tell this is "3 more titles".
- (Readability, Minor) Form dots (filled/empty circles, 2-phone/01, all rows) carry win/loss meaning without any legend on the page; filled vs hollow is not self-evident (which is a win?).
- (Responsiveness, Minor) 4-desktop/01 and 4-desktop/awkward-01: at 1280 the ranking column is ~585 px, below the 600 px container threshold, so it drops the "mecze" and "% wygranych" columns that the narrower 820 tablet (3-tablet/01) shows. Desktop shows less than tablet.
- (Consistency, Nit) Potato column headers "Ziemniaki teraz / Dni jako ziemniak" start at x=20 while every other block starts at 16 (03-league-potato.json lefts L20, all sizes; desktop L276 vs L272).
- (Consistency, Minor) Potato "Pozostali" rows keep an empty 30 px place column (no number, no icon), so their avatars float right of the list's edge and the list looks unranked/indented (2-phone/03 crop rows Bartek..Ewa).
- (IA, Minor) Duets: the explanation "Duety z co najmniej 3 wspólnymi meczami są na górze..." sits at the very bottom, after the folded "Za mało meczów razem..." section (2-phone/02), i.e. after the list it explains.
- (Robustness, Minor) awkward: tournament name cut to "Wielki Jesienny Turniej o ..." / "Wielki Jesienny Tu..." (1-phone-small/awkward-01, truncated audit) and "Małgor..." in the running-game card at 360 px with a stray gap before "&" (1-phone-small/awkward-01). Names that carry identity are cut while there is spare width on the right ("Otwórz" label).
- (Responsiveness, Major candidate, counted under tap targets later) "Elo" segment 39x40 at 360 px (1-phone-small/awkward-02/03 small audit).

## Group 2: games list and players (04, 08, awkward-04, awkward-06)

Files: */04-games.png, */08-players.png, {1-phone-small,2-phone,4-desktop}/awkward-04-games.png, awkward-06-players.png

Good: each game is a compact, symmetric row (red team right-aligned, LED score, blue team), winners bold white and losers grey (8.1:1 grey on black, still readable), running/paused status pills ("Trwa" white on #d0433a 4.62:1, "Wstrzymany"), day headings in natural Polish ("Wczoraj, piątek, 16 października"). 15:13 fits the LED panel. Players page is a clean alphabetical list with "jeszcze bez meczów" for players without games and a "Ty" tag.

Problems:

- (Responsiveness, Minor) 4-desktop/04-games and 4-desktop/awkward-04-games: the two-column grid puts a day's heading only over the left column and reads left-to-right, so the right column is a stack of games without dates; days with an odd count leave holes ("Dziś" one game left / nothing right; "Czwartek 15" 3 games). When one cell has an extra meta line (tournament name wraps, "Do 8 · 5 min"), the dividers of the two columns no longer line up (awkward-04 desktop, rows Czwartek 15 / Środa 14), so the page looks ragged.
- (Robustness, Minor) 1-phone-small/awkward-04-games: the 57-character tournament name in a game's meta wraps to two lines and the second line ("Piłeczkę") is left-aligned while the rest of the row is centred; an orphan word.
- (Consistency, Nit) The tournament name is appended to the time with only a double space ("o 05:01 Turniej piątkowy", 2-phone/04, 4-desktop/04), while the mode uses " · " ("o 03:43 Do 8 · 5 min"): two separators in one meta line.
- (Readability, Nit) Doto's zero is a narrow pointed lozenge, half the width of the 8: "8:0" reads as "8:◊" in the small LED score (2-phone/awkward-04 crop "8:0" rows; zoomed crop z-zero.png).
- (Responsiveness, Nit) 4-desktop/awkward-06-players: the alphabetical list is laid out in two columns read row-wise (Anna, Bo / Grzegorz, Jędrzej ...), so scanning a column skips every other letter.
- Not counted: a game row with a grey fill (first game of yesterday in 2-phone/04 and awkward-04) is the :hover style, probably left by the Tour's pointer.

## Group 3: league statistics (05, awkward-05)

Files: */05-league-stats.png, {1-phone-small,2-phone,4-desktop}/awkward-05-stats.png

Good: clear section headings at one size (Archivo 800 1.25rem), number tiles, records as label/value rows with the game as a link and a date, long double-barrelled names wrap instead of being cut (awkward-05 records), one-hue bar chart, Elo chart with one emphasised line. Two whole-section columns on desktop.

Problems:

- (IA, Minor) The jump chips at the top list only 4 of 8 sections (W liczbach, Kiedy gramy, Rekordy ligi, Elo w czasie; missing Tytuły, Mecze w miesiącach, Stół, Czas goli), and on desktop their order does not match the page: "Kiedy gramy" is chip 2 but the last block of the left column, below Tytuły and the monthly chart (4-desktop/awkward-05-stats; 2-phone/05 crops).
- (Visual system, Minor) Scores are LED digits everywhere else (games list, now card, finished game) but plain Archivo here: "Najwyższa wygrana 8:0", "Najwięcej goli w meczu 15:13", "Największy powrót 7:12" (2-phone/05 crop 2, 4-desktop/05). The same kind of number changes face between screens.
- (IA, Minor) "Kiedy gramy" is a full section heading with one sentence under it ("Najczęściej: pon., 4:00–5:00 (4 mecze).") and no chart (2-phone/05, 4-desktop/05, awkward-05): a heading that promises more than it gives; it is also a jump chip.
- (Responsiveness, Minor) 4-desktop/05 and awkward-05: the "W liczbach" 3-column tile grid leaves an empty cell (row 3: two tiles), and the two "Czas goli" tiles take ~320 of 480 px while their labels wrap ("średni czas do 1. / gola", "średnio między / golami").
- (Readability, Nit) 4-desktop/awkward-05 "Zgrany duet": the line wraps so the next line starts with the separator "· najwyższy procent ...".
- Tap targets: the 18 "small" entries are 39 px tall name/game links inside record lines (audit 05-league-stats.json); these are links in running text and 1 px under the 40 px line; not counted.

## Group 4: tournaments list, tournament page, league sheet and app menu (06, 07, 22, 23, awkward-10/11/13)

Files: */06-tournaments.png, */07-tournament.png, */22-app-menu.png, _/23-league-switcher.png, {1-phone-small,2-phone,4-desktop}/awkward-1[013]-_.png

Good: tournament page has one yellow "Nowy mecz" and the bottom-bar "+" goes quiet (grey) on that page, so there is exactly one ball; table with yellow leader place; summary tiles; the tournament's games reuse the games-list row. Desktop puts table + players beside summary + games. Sheets have a consistent close disc top right and a dark scrim.

Problems:

- (Robustness, Major) 1-phone-small/awkward-10-tournament: the tournament table uses full names and breaks a surname mid-word, leaving a single letter on its own line: "Grzegorz / Brzęczyszczykiewic / z" (row 2) and the same for "Krzysztof- ... / z" (row 8); rows grow to three lines. At 412 px the same rows take two or three lines ("Żaneta / Ślęczkowska- / Wójtowicz", "Małgorzata / Zielińska- / Nowakowska", 1-phone-small crop 1). The ranking solves this with short names on phones; the tournament table does not.
- (Robustness, Minor) 4-desktop/awkward-10-tournament, 2-phone/awkward-10: "W grze (8)" lists 8 players while the table above has 13 rows, and the leader (Piotr Wiśniewski) plus Kasia, Ola, Zbigniew, Tomasz are not "in play". Players who left are never named as such, so the table and the list contradict each other without explanation.
- (Consistency, Minor) Summary names: in league statistics the record holders are underlined links (2-phone/05 "Ewa · najwyższe Elo"); in the tournament summary the same kind of line is plain grey text ("Najlepszy strzelec 10 / Celina, Darek", 2-phone/07 crop 1, 4-desktop/07). The game links in the summary are 15 px tall (07-tournament.json small: 186x15) while the same links in league statistics get a 39 px line.
- (IA, Minor) On phones the league's settings are reachable only from the league title's chevron sheet (23-league-switcher, awkward-13: "Zaproś", "Ustawienia ligi", "Zmień ligę"); the hamburger on the same header opens a different sheet ("Aplikacja": language, "Którym graczem jesteś?", "Ranking ogólny", 22-app-menu). Two menus side by side in one header, and the chevron reads as "switch league", not "league menu". The desktop rail has "Ustawienia ligi" directly, the phone bar does not.
- (Consistency, Minor) The league sheet mixes two list styles in one list: filled tonal cards ("Zaproś: udostępnij link do ligi", "Ustawienia ligi") and plain divided rows ("Wyjazd w góry", "Wszystkie ligi") (2-phone/23-league-switcher, 2-phone/awkward-13).
- (Robustness, Nit) The 62-character league name is cut in the sheet header and in the header at 360 px ("Międzynarodowa Liga Piłkarzyków Działu...", 1-phone-small/awkward-01, 2-phone/awkward-13); the full name is never visible at 360 px outside settings.
- (Responsiveness, Nit) 4-desktop/06-tournaments: one 490 px card in the left half of a 1000 px column; with one tournament the two-column grid leaves the page three-quarters empty.

## Group 5: settings, profile (overview, stats, games), kit editor, comparison (10-15, awkward-07/08/09)

Files: */10-settings.png, */11-profile.png, */12-profile-stats.png, */13-profile-games.png, */14-kit-editor.png, _/15-compare.png, {1-phone-small,2-phone,4-desktop}/awkward-0[789]-_.png

Good: settings are short, grouped (Ogólne / Ranking / Ważne zmiany), the destructive entry is red (#ff8a80 on card 7.79:1) and says it can be undone. The profile header card carries everything that matters (league, position, titles, "To ja", "Koszulka", Elo with delta, W–L, %), the Overview/Statistics/Games switch is clear, games show the player on a chip with W/P tiles and Elo change. The kit editor is a real, playful editor with a live preview and one yellow "Zapisz koszulkę". Compare has a clear structure (chance, head to head in LED, together, Elo chart in two validated colours, shared games).

Problems:

- (Responsiveness, Blocker) 4-desktop/15-compare and 4-desktop/awkward-09-compare: "Wspólne mecze" sits in the right half and still uses the games list's own two-column grid, so each game gets ~225 px and the names are cut to "Bart…", "Celi…" (normal league, 5 truncations) and "Kr…", "Łuk…", "Zbi…", "Mał…", "Żan…" (awkward, 23 truncations in 4-desktop/awkward-09-compare.json). Who played is the whole content of a game row; it is unreadable at the most common laptop width. The second sub-column holds a stray game now and then (Czwartek 15 / Środa 7 / Niedziela 27 września), so the column looks broken. At 1920 (5-wide/15-compare) names fit but the same orphan cells remain.
- (Readability, Minor) Partners/rivals tables cut names with an ellipsis ("Żaneta Ślęczkowska-…", "Grzegorz Brzęczyszc…", "Małgorzata Zielińska-…", 1-phone-small/awkward-08 crops 3-4, 7 truncations) while the tournament table wraps them and the ranking shortens them to first names: three strategies for the same long name.
- (Responsiveness, Minor) Partner/rival names are the only tap targets of those rows and are 17 px tall (12-profile-stats.json: 12 links 31-48x17; awkward-08: 24 links). In league statistics the same kind of link got a 39 px line.
- (Visual system, Minor) The profile avatar has a 3 px ball-yellow ring (2-phone/11, 4-desktop/11, all profiles) that means nothing; yellow is reserved for the main action and for selection (kit editor), so the ring reads as "selected" or "tap me".
- (Responsiveness, Minor) 4-desktop/11-profile: the header stats (1520 / 13–10 / 57%) are spread over a 990 px card, the Overview/Statistics/Games switch is 990 px wide, and the four "Rekordy" tiles are 490 px wide with a 2-digit number at the left; the profile is a stretched phone layout on desktop (5-wide/11 the same).
- (Visual system, Minor) Compare colours: player 2 is #d95926 (orange, hue ~17°) right next to red-team red #d0433a (hue ~4°) and the red LED digits of the shared games below (2-phone/15, 4-desktop/15). "Bartek" in orange and the red team are easy to conflate; the teal/orange pair was chosen to avoid blue but now collides with red.
- (Consistency, Nit) "Przeciw sobie 5 : 3" puts spaces around the colon; every other score is "8:5" (2-phone/15).
- (Consistency, Nit) Profile statistics: the two Elo tiles' labels are bold ("Elo w obronie (13 meczów 2 na 2)") while every other tile label is regular (2-phone/12 crop 0, 4-desktop/12).
- (Robustness, Nit) 1-phone-small/awkward-07: the league in the profile card is cut to "Międzynar…" next to "ze wszystkich lig ›", so at 360 px you cannot tell which league the numbers are from.
- (IA, Nit) Opening a profile from the ranking highlights the "Gracze" tab in the bottom bar (2-phone/11, 13), so the bar no longer shows where the user came from.

## Group 6: game screens (27 running, 28 paused, 16 finished, awkward-12/14; all sizes)

Files: */27-game-running.png, */28-game-paused.png, _/16-game-finished.png, {1-phone-small,2-phone,4-desktop}/awkward-1[24]-_.png

Good: the strongest part of the app. The drawn table is the control: rods are huge tap zones, team rails take the team colours, name plates sit on the rails with the player's goals in LED, the score boards are LED panels, the clock is LED. Portrait (phones, tablet) and landscape (desktop, wide) both hold, also with 15:13 and long names (the long name moves to "Ostatnio: Gol z obrony: Krzysztof-Maksymilian…", which may be cut). The pause overlay is a model dialog: big state, score, yellow "Wznów", "Wyjdź, dokończę później", red "Usuń mecz". The finished game puts the result on the felt with Elo changes as green/red pills, then a step chart and a goal log; "Rewanż" (or "Wróć do turnieju") is the yellow action.

Problems:

- (Consistency, Minor) The game screen's controls change label and place between sizes: "kto strzelił" (phones) vs "Zapis: kto strzelił" (tablet, desktop); the rotate button sits next to back on the tablet, in the side panel on desktop and is absent from the 412 px phone header; on desktop/wide the back arrow is inside the right panel at x≈995/1460 instead of the top-left corner every other page uses (2-phone/27, 3-tablet/27, 4-desktop/27, 5-wide/27).
- (Consistency, Minor) 4-desktop/16-game-finished and awkward-12: the rail's league section loses "Ustawienia ligi" on this page, and the content column starts at x=408 instead of x=272 used by every league page, so the left edge jumps when a game is opened from the games list.
- (Visual system, Nit) Paused: the hint "Dotknij drążka gracza, który strzelił." stays at full strength under the pause overlay while the controls next to it are dimmed (2-phone/28, 1-phone-small/28, 4-desktop/28): it tells the user to do something the pause forbids.
- (Readability, Nit) 2-phone/awkward-12: the long name is cut with two dots "Brzęczyszczyki.." instead of an ellipsis; 1-phone-small/16: "· 7 min / 15 s gry" breaks inside the duration.
- (Robustness, Nit) 1-phone-small/awkward-14: the blue defender's rail plate is cut to "Małgor…" while "Małgorzata 3" fits on the board above; fine as a fallback.

## Group 7: start flows, leagues, overall views, empty state (19-26, 17, 18, 20, 21)

Files: */19-empty-league.png, */20-leagues.png, */21-new-league.png, */24-new-play.png, */25-new-game.png, */26-new-tournament.png, */17-overall-ranking.png, */18-overall-profile.png

Good: starting a game is "+" → "Mecz" (highlighted as the main choice with a yellow tile) → "Zacznij mecz": 3 taps, teams pre-filled from the last game, rules as chips/toggles with a plain-language hint ("Od 7:7 gra toczy się, aż ktoś prowadzi dwoma golami, choćby 90:88."), and a summary with Elo win chance right above the yellow button. The new-tournament sheet explains every format with its minimum size. The empty league has one obvious first step ("Zagrajcie pierwszy mecz", yellow "Zacznij mecz") and the bottom "+" goes quiet. New league is a 2-step wizard with an example name. Polish copy is natural and consistent throughout.

Problems:

- (IA, Minor) "Wszystkie ligi" (rail, league sheet) opens a page titled "Ligi" whose only list is "Na tym urządzeniu" plus a search field (2-phone/20, 4-desktop/20, 5-wide/20). The label promises every league; the page shows the device's own and makes you search for the rest, and on desktop it repeats the rail's list verbatim.
- (Readability, Minor) Kit editor: the black colour swatch is #1a1a1a on the #16181a sheet (1.02:1) with a 15 % white ring (#3c3c3c, 1.61:1); a colour option's boundary should reach 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11). It almost disappears (2-phone/14, 4-desktop/14, both colour rows).
- (Robustness, Nit) 2-phone/19-empty-league: the four players are shown as faces only (three kits look alike, "CE"), with no names, so "who is in this league" cannot be read.
- (IA, Nit) The overall profile (18) shows league titles ("Mur", "Weteran", ...) under "Wszystkie ligi" without saying which league they come from.

## 2. The ten most important problems

1. **Desktop comparison: shared games are unreadable (Blocker).** Where: 4-desktop/15-compare, 4-desktop/awkward-09-compare (28 truncations), 5-wide/15-compare (orphan cells). What: "Wspólne mecze" sits in the right half of a 2-column page and still applies the games list's own 2-column grid (`.fl-wide-page fl-game-list .games` in styles.scss), so each game is ~225 px wide and names become "Kr…", "Bart…". Why: who played is the whole content of the row; the page is useless at the most common laptop width. Fix: drive the games grid by its container (`@container (min-width: 900px)`) instead of the viewport, or render shared games full width below the two columns.
2. **Tap targets under 40 px (Major).** Where: "Elo" segment 39x40 at 360 (1-phone-small/awkward-02/03); partner/rival names 17 px tall (*/12, _/awkward-08: 24 links); tournament summary links 15 px tall (_/07). Why: a phone used standing at the table; these are the only way into a comparison or a game. Fix: make whole table rows the link (min-height 44 px); reuse the 12 px block padding the league stats already give to name links; let the view switch scroll or trim padding so every segment is ≥ 44 px wide.
3. **Tournament table breaks names mid-word (Major).** Where: 1-phone-small/awkward-10 ("Brzęczyszczykiewic / z"), 2-phone/awkward-10 (3-line rows). Why: broken words look like a bug and the table becomes 3x taller. Fix: use the ranking's short-name rule (first name, plus an initial on collision) whenever the table is narrower than ~440 px; never allow `overflow-wrap: anywhere` on names.
4. **One rule for long names.** Where: ranking Elo view shows "Grzegorz", the potato view of the same list "Grzegorz Brzęczysz…", partners "Grzegorz Brzęczyszc…", tournament table wraps, records wrap (1-phone-small/awkward-01, -03, -08, -10, 2-phone/awkward-05). Why: the same person looks different on every screen and cut names lose meaning. Fix: a single display-name helper (short form below a container width, full form above, ellipsis only as a last resort), used by every list and table.
5. **Ranking row semantics are unexplained.** Where: */01, */awkward-01, */17. What: "+1/+3" title-count pills look like Elo changes (and lose their outline on the "me" row, ~1:1), and the five ●○ dots have no legend. Why: the ranking is the home screen; a newcomer misreads it. Fix: show hidden titles as "…" or a small stacked-icon count without a plus sign, give the pill an outline of ≥ 3:1 on every row, and label the dots once ("Ostatnie 5: ● wygrana ○ porażka") in a column header or under the switch.
6. **The 1280 desktop is a stretched phone in several places.** Where: 4-desktop/01 (ranking drops columns the tablet shows), 4-desktop/04 (ragged 2-column games), 4-desktop/11-12 (profile spread over 990 px, overview in 2 columns only at 1920), 4-desktop/05 (tile holes). Why: desktop shows less than the tablet and looks unfinished. Fix: narrow the right "now" column to ~300 px so the ranking passes its 600 px threshold (or lower the threshold to 560); start the profile's 2-column overview at 1000 px; use `auto-fit` tiles with spans so rows fill; make day headings span both game columns and flow games column-first.
7. **LED digits are not used consistently for scores.** Where: records "8:0", "15:13", "7:12", profile "Najwyższa wygrana 8:0", tournament summary "8:1" are Archivo (*/05, */07, */11), while every game row, board and the finished game use Doto LED. Why: the LED face is the app's main visual signal for "a score"; it should mean one thing. Fix: apply `.fl-led` to score values in records and tiles (or state that LED is only for live/game objects and also move the "Przeciw sobie 5 : 3" tally to Archivo).
8. **League settings are hidden behind the title on phones; two header menus.** Where: */22-app-menu, */23-league-switcher, */awkward-13. Why: the chevron reads as "switch league"; a user looking for settings opens the hamburger and finds language and "Którym graczem jesteś?" instead. Fix: one menu with two sections ("Ta liga": zaproś, ustawienia, zmień ligę; "Aplikacja": język, kim jestem, ranking ogólny), or add "Ustawienia ligi" to the Players tab.
9. **Tournament players list contradicts the table.** Where: */awkward-10 ("W grze (8)" vs 13 rows; the leader is not "in play"). Why: users cannot tell whether the leader left, was removed, or the list is wrong. Fix: list departed players under "Wypisani (5)" and fade them in the table with a "wypisany" tag.
10. **At 360 px the ranking is below the fold.** Where: 1-phone-small/01, 1-phone-small/awkward-01: identity card, "Teraz w lidze", paused game and tournament cards push the first ranking row to y≈440-500 of 640; one row is visible above the bottom bar. Why: "see where you stand" is the home screen's job. Fix: collapse the "now" cards into one compact strip on small screens (one line per game, tournament as a chip), or move the tournament card below the ranking.

## 3. Overall

Overall score **4.0** (lowest dimension: Responsiveness). Mean 6.25.

The phone experience is close to good: the game screen, the start flow (3 taps), the pause dialog, the finished game and the new-game dialog are the work of a careful designer, and token contrast is excellent everywhere (secondary text 6.1-8.1:1, team text 5.6-6.8:1, CTA 11.6:1). What keeps the score down is one broken desktop screen, sub-40 px targets, and a long tail of inconsistencies in how names, scores, menus and main actions are presented from screen to screen.

## 4. What I could not judge

- Anything in motion or on interaction: tap feedback, the finish panel's 8 s "Dalej" countdown and confetti, snackbars and undo after deleting, the transition into the finished game, focus states and keyboard use, screen-reader labels.
- Screens not in the Tour: the "Jak czytać ranking" help sheet, the player picker opened from a lineup slot, "Wybierz" (which player am I), the goal-detail modes (rod / figure taps, the "turn the phone" prompt), a phone in landscape or lying flat, cup brackets, king-of-the-table and draw formats, finished tournaments and the winners table, the timeline / "Against the league" sheet, archived and deleted leagues, loading skeletons, offline and error states, step 2 of the new-league wizard, the English translation.
- The grey fill on one game row (2-phone/04, awkward-04) and the first overall-ranking row (2-phone/17) is the :hover style, probably left by the Tour's pointer; I did not count it, but on touch screens a sticky hover after a tap is worth checking.
- Full-page shots hide one row behind the fixed bottom bar (e.g. Gosia in 2-phone/01); treated as an artefact as the brief says.
