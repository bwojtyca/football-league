## 2-phone: league ranking, duets, potato, games, statistics

- 01-league-ranking: clear hierarchy (who-are-you card, live game on an LED board, tournament card, segmented switch, list). Title icons after names (star, handshake, shield, medal, potato, target, flame) carry meaning but are unlabelled on the list; meaning only via the "?" help. The form-dot legend ("Kropki: ostatnie 5 meczów") sits below the list, out of first view. Avatars mix kit figures and two-letter initials ("FI", "CE"), two visual languages in one column. The live game's clock "04:23" is LED digits at roughly 13-14 px (below the "LED from about 18 px" rule of the look), the dot-matrix is hard to read at that size.
- 02-league-duets: overlapping avatars hide the initials of the first avatar ("CE" reads "CI", "FI" reads "F") on rows 4, 9, 11. The fold "Za mało meczów razem (mniej niż 3), bez miejsca (7)" wraps and leaves its chevron alone on a second line. The same pair metric is "71%" here and "71,4%" in Statystyki > Zgrany duet.
- 03-league-potato: the current potatoes' meta line is cut: "13 meczów · ziemniak od 12…", "12 meczów · ziemniak od 14…" (audit `truncated`), the date they became potato is lost even with 5-letter names at 412 px. The right column repeats "dni jako ziemniak" in every row (noise).
- 04-games: good row design (LED score, winner in ink, loser in ink-2, day headings). The Doto zero renders as a hollow diamond ("◊:8"), shutouts read oddly. "Statystyki ligi" card is duplicated at the top of Mecze and the bottom of Ranking; on Mecze its subtitle wraps with an orphan "czasie". The grey row (14 Oct, 8:5) is the hover style (`.game:hover`), a Tour artefact, not counted.
- 05-league-stats: chips "W liczbach / Kiedy gramy / Rekordy ligi / Elo w cz…" scroll horizontally with a fade (acceptable). Tile grid leaves a hole (row 3: two tiles and an empty cell). The Elo chart has no x axis (no dates), so "Elo w czasie" shows no time. Heat map has no scale numbers ("jaśniej znaczy więcej"). Names inside the Titles and Records meta lines are 15 px tall links (audit `small`, 18 entries); they are the only way to open those games/profiles from here.

## 2-phone: tournaments, tournament, players, settings, profile, kit, compare, finished game

- 06-tournaments: one running tournament and a tonal "Nowy turniej"; 75 % of the screen empty, but correct for one item. Unlike "Gracze" (heading "Gracze 7" plus a hint), Ranking / Mecze / Turnieje have no page heading under the league title: three of four tabs start differently.
- 07-tournament: header has both a kebab (tournament actions) and the hamburger (app menu) side by side, two "more" icons with different meaning (also 16-game-finished has kebab only, other pages hamburger only). The FAB turns grey here so "Nowy mecz" is the only yellow (good). Table "Gole" column "22:12" and compare "17:19 gole" use the same mm:ss-looking colon notation as durations ("08:28"), goals for:against read like times. "W grze (4)" repeats "Wypisz" with an icon on every row (noise for a secondary action).
- 08-players: alphabetical list with Elo, fine. The Players tab largely duplicates the Ranking (same people, same Elo).
- 10-settings: the league name field has no visible save affordance (autosave not signalled). The switch is 52x32 (audit `small`), but it sits in a full-width row.
- 11/12/13-profile: the profile card (avatar, "Biuro 2026 · profil ogólny" link, "To ja", titles, "Koszulka", three numbers) takes about 345 CSS px, so on every sub-tab content starts below the middle of a 412x915 screen. Three different number+label patterns for stats: league tiles = number over label (05), profile "Rekordy" tiles = label over number (11), profile "Statystyki" = bare lowercase label over number, no tile (12). Labels switch between sentence case ("Mecze", "Czas gry") and lowercase ("strzelone", "samobóje", "mecze"). "Elo w obronie (13 meczów 2 na 2)" breaks as "2 / na 2)". In profile games bold marks both the winners and the profile's player, so in a loss "Ala" is bold on the losing side (13-profile-games, also 15-compare): bold no longer means "won". Partners/rivals names are 17 px tall links (audit `small`, 12 entries).
- 14-kit-editor: clear, yellow "Zapisz koszulkę". The shirt number is shown in yellow LED (yellow outside the main action). Close button is an X in a grey circle here, in 25-new-game and 26-new-tournament, but a bare X in 21-new-league.
- 15-compare: "Zmień" only under the second player (asymmetric). "50% wygranych · 2–" / "2" breaks a score across lines. The two-player colours (blue #3987e5 vs orange #d95926) sit next to a team-red/blue vocabulary used everywhere else; the "Szansa" bar looks like a red-vs-blue team bar though neither player is on a team colour.
- 16-game-finished: strong (felt card, LED score, Elo deltas, step chart). The running score in the goal log is LED at about 13-14 px (below the 18 px rule), zeros as diamonds. Plain disc bullets for the two facts are a one-off list style. No title in the header (back, yellow "Rewanż", kebab).

## 2-phone: overall, empty league, leagues, sheets, dialogs, game screen

- 17-overall-ranking / 18-overall-profile: consistent with the league versions; no bottom bar outside a league (fine). Row 1 grey = hover artefact.
- 19-empty-league: good empty state ("Zagrajcie pierwszy mecz", yellow "Zacznij mecz", FAB greyed so one yellow).
- 20-leagues: no app name or one-line purpose on this page; a first-timer who lands here (or anyone sharing a phone) sees "Ligi" and a search box only (a welcome screen exists in pl.json `welcome.*` but is not in the Tour). Crest "WWG" fills its tile edge to edge.
- 22-app-menu: sheet "Aplikacja" (language, which player am I, overall ranking). Fine; its left edge is 20 px vs 16 px on pages.
- 23-league-switcher: crest letter yellow here, white on the leagues page.
- 24-new-play: clear three choices, Mecz highlighted. Starting a game = "+", "Mecz", "Zacznij mecz": 3 taps, meets the owner's rule.
- 25-new-game: clear, lineups on felt, odds on an LED panel, yellow "Zacznij mecz". Spacing uneven: about 50 px gap between "Zasady" and its chips, about 90 px of nothing between "Ekran i zapis goli" and the summary panel, and the felt is half empty under the four slots. Two 52x32 switches (audit `small`).
- 26-new-tournament: clear format list with needs ("od 3 osób").
- 27-game-running: the drawn table is the input, but once a goal is in, nothing on screen says what to tap ("Dotknij drążka gracza, który strzelił" shows only before the first goal; afterwards the bar shows "Ostatnio"). A player picking up a running game sees rods and rail plates with vertical names, no affordance. The clock is yellow LED (yellow = main action elsewhere). "Zamień pozycje" is two dot-only buttons (red dot, blue dot) whose meaning depends on a small grey label.
- 28-game-paused: good overlay (Wznów yellow, leave, delete). Under it "Cofnij" and the swap dots stay at full strength while "Samobój…" is dimmed, so it is unclear what still works during a pause.

## 2-phone: awkward league

- awkward-01-ranking: the you-card and the "Ty" pill in the list work well. Long names lose their surnames everywhere a row has form dots: "Grzegorz Brzęczy…", "Krzysztof-…" (only the first half of a hyphenated first name survives), "Małgorzata Zieliń…", "Żaneta Ślęczkows…". The live card cuts all four names ("Krzysztof-… & Żaneta Ślę… / Małgorzata… & Grzegorz …"), the tournament card cuts "Wielki Jesienny Turniej o …". Audit `truncated`: 10 entries. "Xi" has an unexplained trend icon (title "Król powrotów", explained only in Statystyki).
- awkward-02-duets: pair names cut to first names or less ("Małgorzat… & Zbigniew", "Piotr Wiśn… & Grzegorz …"); 25+ entries in `truncated`. The overlapping second avatar hides the first's initials ("XI" reads "X(").
- awkward-03-potato: meta cut on all three current potatoes ("ziemniak od 12…", "od 14 p…"). The order is current potatoes first (3, 6, 8 days) then the rest by days (7, 6, 4…), with no rank numbers or sort hint: 7 days below 3 days looks like a sorting error.
- awkward-04-games: game rows cut names ("Krzysztof-Maks…", "Małgorzata Zie…") but stay readable; the paused game shows a "Wstrzymany" pill, good. Durations and goal tallies use the same "19:02 / 15:13" shapes as start times.

"Z 120 meczów z zapisem goli (od października 2026)" contradicts the months chart that shows those games in August and September. "Najdłuższy mecz 19:53" is mm:ss but looks like a time of day, next to game rows whose "19:02" is a time of day.
- awkward-06-players: "jeszcze bez meczów" instead of an Elo for players without games: good.
- awkward-07/08-profile: two-line name in the header works; league name cut to "Międzynarodow…" in the card; partners/rivals cut with ellipsis, fine.
- awkward-09-compare: "33% wygranych · 2–" / "4" breaks a score across lines again; long name wraps the "ostatnie mecze" line with the dots far right.
- awkward-10-tournament: the table wraps long names on two lines (good); players with 0 games get no place number (good).
- awkward-12-game-finished: "Grzegorz / Brzęczyszczyki.." (two dots, a clipped ellipsis) in the felt card; yellow "Wróć do turnieju" replaces "Rewanż" (consistent placement).
- awkward-14-game-running: the top and bottom score boards grow with the names and reach 4 px from the screen edges (20 px in 27-game-running), so the board width depends on data. Rail plates are vertical and cut ("Krzysztof-Maksy…", "Małgorzata Zielin…").

## 4-desktop (1280x800), ordinary and awkward

- Rail (all league pages): the league's sections (Ranking / Mecze / Turnieje / Gracze) are drawn as centred icon-over-label tiles with 12 px labels, while the leagues above them are left-aligned rows with the icon on the left and 15 px labels. Cause: `league-layout.component.ts` `.tab` (grid, centred, `font: 700 0.75rem`) overrides the global `.fl-rail .tab` row style. The main desktop navigation looks like a phone bottom bar pasted into the rail (01..13, 19, awkward-01..11).
- "Nowa rozgrywka" in the rail turns white-filled when the page has its own yellow action (07-tournament, 19-empty-league, awkward-10): a white filled pill is the second loudest thing on screen and competes with the yellow; on the phone the same "quiet" state is dark grey.
- 01-league-ranking: good two-column use (ranking + "you/now/tournament" column). Elsewhere at 1280 (04, 05, 06, 08, 10, 11..15, 20) the page is one 720 px column ending at x=992, the right 288 px empty, while the header's gear and menu sit at x≈1200-1270, detached from the column they belong to.
- 05-league-stats: "Czas goli" tiles are 113 px wide in a 720 px column, so "średnio / między / golami" wraps on three lines; heat-map labels sit 200 px away from their cells.
- 15-compare: "Razem w drużynie" three 113 px tiles, then 360 px of nothing; "wygranych · / 2–2" wraps.
- 16-game-finished / awkward-12: the game page drops the league's sections and "Nowa rozgrywka" from the rail and centres the content (left edge 408 instead of 272, audit `lefts`), so the left edge jumps between pages and the current league is not highlighted.
- 27/28-game-running (landscape): the table and panel work; the back arrow sits in the panel at x≈995, y≈200 (every other page: top-left). The colon between the panel's LED digits is two barely lit dots.
- awkward-01/02/03: the side column at 1280 is narrower than a phone, so the live card cuts names harder than on the phone ("Krzyszt… & Żaneta … / Małgorz… & Grzegor…"), and the tournament card reads "Wielki Jesienny Tur…".
- Crests: "WWG" overflows its 32 px rail crest (20-leagues, 19-empty-league); "MLP" fits.
- Dialogs and sheets open as centred panels (21-26): good. New league close is a bare X; kit, new game, new tournament use an X in a grey circle.

## Ten most important problems

1. **Desktop rail navigation is drawn with the phone's bottom-bar style** (Consistency, Major). 4-desktop/01-13, 19, awkward-*, 5-wide/*. The league's sections are centred icon-over-12 px-label tiles under left-aligned league rows with 15 px labels; the main navigation of every desktop page looks broken. Cause: `.tab` in `src/app/league/league-layout.component.ts` (grid, centred, `font: 700 0.75rem`) overrides the global `.fl-rail .tab`. Fix: scope the component rule to `.tabs .tab` (bottom bar only) so the rail uses its row style (icon left, 0.95 rem label, 44 px rows, active = card-2 background with a yellow icon).
2. **Game screen controls lose their labels at 360 px** (IA, Major). 1-phone-small/27, 28, awkward-14. The goal-detail pill becomes a bare person icon and "Zamień pozycje" disappears, leaving two dot-only buttons, on the screen used most. Fix: never drop the text: shorten instead ("Gol: kto", "Zamień ⇅" inside each dot button, or one "Zamień pozycje" button that opens a two-option sheet); if space is short, move the detail pill into the kebab menu with its label.
3. **Long names cut to fragments in the live card, duets and on the game screen** (Robustness, Major). 1-phone-small/awkward-01, 2-phone/awkward-02 and -14, 4-desktop/awkward-01..03. "Krzyszt… & Żaneta … / Małgor… & Grzego…" says little about who is playing. Fix: one `displayName()` for compact places: the first name when unique in the league, else first name + surname initial ("Grzegorz B."); let each team take two lines in the live card; widen the desktop side column (or put the live card above the ranking) so it is never narrower than a phone.
4. **No guidance for the main action after the first goal** (IA, Minor; Hierarchy). 2-phone/27, awkward-14, 4-desktop/27. The hint "Dotknij drążka gracza, który strzelił" exists only before the first goal; afterwards the screen shows rods, rotated 12 px rail names and a yellow clock as the loudest element. Fix: keep a short persistent cue (for example a faint "dotknij drążka" line in the "Ostatnio" bar or player names at each team's handles at 14 px), and render the clock in ink, not the action yellow.
5. **Header and menu patterns differ from page to page** (Consistency, Minor; IA). 2-phone/07 (kebab next to hamburger), 16 (kebab only), 4-desktop/27 (back arrow mid-right in the panel), 22/23 (league switching only behind the title). Fix: one header: back at the left, title, at most one overflow menu at the right that holds page actions and, below a divider, the app items (language, who am I, overall ranking, all leagues).
6. **Ambiguous number formats** (Readability and Consistency, Minor). 05, 07, 15, 16, awkward-04/05. "19:53" (a duration) sits next to "19:02" (a start time); goals "22:12" and "17:19 gole" look like clock times; durations appear as "05:50", "7 min 15 s" and "2 h 55 min"; "71%" vs "71,4%". Fix: durations always as "8 min 28 s" / "2 h 55 min", goal tallies as "22–12", colon only for live or final scores, one percent precision.
