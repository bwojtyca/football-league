## Working notes (appended per screen group)

### Method

- Phone shots are 2x (824 px wide for 412 css px). Long shots cropped into 1500 px segments (own crops in scratchpad/eval6-crops, made only from eval6/ files) to see detail.
- Contrast computed with the WCAG formula from the hex values in src/styles.scss:
  - ink #f2f4f0 on paper #000 18.97, on card #16181b 16.07, on card-2 #202328 14.24
  - ink-2 #9aa39d on paper 8.10, card 6.86, card-2 6.08, dialog 6.87, sheet 7.12, board 7.60, **felt #0d5a31 3.21**
  - switch inactive #b9c2bc on card-2 8.63; on-ball #1b1410 on ball #ffc629 11.58
  - red-text #ff5a4e on paper 6.82 / card 5.78; blue-text #5c8cff on paper 6.65 / card 5.63 / card-2 4.99
  - white on red fill #d0433a **4.62** (just passes), white on blue fill #3a64d6 5.28
  - white on felt centre #157a42 5.39, on felt #0d5a31 8.32
  - win #5be394 on card 10.89, loss #ff8a80 on card 7.79, chart #3fb36e on paper 7.88
  - series-1 #2bb3a0 on card 6.83, series-2 #d95926 on card 4.58
  - line #232623 on paper 1.37 / on card 1.16 (row dividers, card outlines: decorative only); line-2 #4a4f4b on card 2.13 (form-field outline: below the 3:1 non-text threshold for a control boundary)
  - Token text pairs all pass 4.5:1; the risks are ink-2 on felt (3.21) and any opacity-reduced text (checked per screen below).

### Group 1: league ranking views, 2-phone (01, 02, 03)

- 01-league-ranking: header (league name + chevron switcher, hamburger), an onboarding card "Którym graczem jesteś? / Wybierz", "Teraz w lidze" live game on an LED board, a tournament card, a segmented switch Elo / % wygranych / Duety / Ziemniak + "?" help, then the list (place, avatar or kit, name, title icon, W–L "· głównie atak", 5 form dots, Elo, last change). Yellow "+" FAB in the bottom bar is the one primary action. lefts: 6 distinct (only the switch's own buttons differ) - clean.
  - Problem (IA/readability): the "+1" / "+3" after a title icon (Ewa star +1, Ala shield +3, Filip potato +1, Darek flame +1) has no label; a newcomer cannot tell if it is "+1 title", "+1 place" or "+1 point". Next to the Elo change (-13 / +12) it reads as another rating delta. Minor.
  - Problem (IA): the 5 form dots (filled/empty) have no visible legend on the screen (only behind "?"). Nit.
  - Problem (system): numbers are not used consistently - the live score is Doto LED, the live clock "04:23" is Archivo, the Elo "1538" is Archivo tabular. The rubric's "LED digits for scores and clocks": the clock on the Teraz card is not LED. Minor (system).
  - Above the fold on 412x915: onboarding card + live game + tournament card + switch push the first ranking row to y ≈ 420 css; acceptable on this size, but see 1-phone-small.
- 02-league-duets: shared places on ties (2,2,4,4 ... 9,9,11,11) are correct. The fold "Za mało meczów razem (mniej niż 3), bez miejsca (7)" wraps leaving "(7)" alone on line 2 - orphan. Minor (readability/polish). The explanation of the order sits _below_ the folded list, far from the ranking it explains. Nit.
- 03-league-potato: "Ziemniaki teraz" highlighted on a brown card (#3a2e1c-like), "Pozostali" list with "Dni jako ziemniak"; column header labels "Ziemniaki teraz"/"Dni jako ziemniak" are small (~13 px) but secondary. The potato rows show the potato icon in the place column instead of a number; "Pozostali" rows have no place number at all - a list of "days as potato" sorted descending without places; fine. "Zasady" fold is collapsed: a newcomer to the potato view sees "Dni jako ziemniak 2" with no idea how one becomes a potato unless they open "Zasady" or "?". Minor (IA).

### 2. The ten most important problems (most severe first)

1. **League sheet header breaks (Blocker, robustness; Major, readability).** Where: awkward-13 (360/412/1280), 23-league-switcher (all sizes). What: title and meta sit on one line - "Biuro 20267 graczy · 30 meczów"; a long name is cut mid-word ("Międzynarodo") with the meta overlapping it and running under the close disc. Why: it is the header of the league's own menu, opened every time someone switches league; it misreads a number and hides the league's name. Fix: put the meta on its own line (the `.about` grid in `league-switcher.component.ts` is not taking effect - make `.about` a block/grid that stacks `<b>` and `<small>`, `small { display:block }`), clamp the name to two lines at word boundaries with an ellipsis, and give the header row `padding-right: 56px` so nothing reaches the close disc.
2. **Names cut in the main ranking and duets on phones (Major, readability + robustness).** Where: 2-phone/awkward-01 ("Krzyszt…", "Tomasz Ołd…"), 1-phone-small/awkward-02 ("Krzys… & Małgo…"), live card "Małgor…". Why: the ranking is the first screen and the name is its key datum. Fix: use the short-name scheme the games list already has (first name, plus surname initial when two share it: "Krzysztof B."), move title icons and the "+N" to the second line, and drop the five form dots below ~440 px (as 360 already does); allow names to wrap to two lines rather than ellipsis.
3. **Game screen header overflows at 412 px (Major, responsiveness).** Where: 2-phone/27, 2-phone/awkward-14. What: back, rotate, "Zapis: kto strzelił" pill, clock, pause and ⋮ do not fit; ⋮ is half off-screen. Why: the menu holds end/remove actions during a live game on the most common phone width. Fix: raise the compact-header breakpoint (the 360 px version) to ~440 px or use a container query on the header; move "rotate" into ⋮ and keep ⋮ a full 48 px target.
4. **New-game dialog hides its summary (Major, responsiveness).** Where: 4-desktop/25 (1280x800), 1-phone-small/25. What: the LED board with "2 na 2 · Do 8 · przewaga 2 · 47% Szansa wygranej 53%" is cut by the sticky "Zacznij mecz". Why: it is the confirmation of what is about to start, on the main flow. Fix: make the summary a compact one-line part of the sticky footer above the button, or reserve the footer's height as bottom padding of the scroll area; on desktop cap the dialog at `max-height: calc(100vh - 48px)` with internal scroll.
5. **Desktop profile tabs are one stretched column (Major, responsiveness).** Where: 4-desktop/12, 13, awkward-08; 5-wide/12, 13. What: positions/colours/partners/rivals tables span ~1000 px (label at the left edge, values 800 px away); tiles fill 2 of 6 slots; games are a centred strip. Why: hard to read across; other pages at this size already use two columns. Fix: give the profile's Statystyki the `.fl-wide-page .fl-stats` two-column treatment (or cap each table at ~520 px and place them two per row), and render Mecze with the league games grid or capped at 720 px.
6. **Unlabelled "+1"/"+3" after title icons (Minor, IA).** Where: 01, 17, awkward-01, 4-desktop/01. Why: next to the Elo delta "+12" it reads as another score change; nothing says it means "more titles". Fix: show up to two title icons and a neutral "+1" chip with an accessible label and tooltip ("i 1 tytuł więcej"), or drop the count from the ranking row and keep the full list on the profile.
7. **Game screen chrome not scaled for tablets and desktops (Minor, responsiveness).** Where: 3-tablet/27, 4-desktop/27, 5-wide/27. What: LED clock ≈16 px, name plates 12-13 px, hint 13 px next to an 800-1430 px table; the device sits by the table and is read standing. Fix: scale the header, plates and panel with the table (e.g. `clamp()` on font sizes keyed to the table width), clock at 28-32 px on tablets.
8. **Game screen bottom panel: unlabelled swaps, live during pause (Minor, IA).** Where: 27, 28, 1-phone-small/27, 4-desktop/27-28. What: "Zamień pozycje"/"Zamień" + two bare colour dots; during pause the hint "Dotknij drążka…" and "Cofnij" stay live while "Samobój…" is dimmed. Fix: label the buttons ("Zamień czerwonych" / "Zamień niebieskich" or a swap icon in the team colour with text); during pause dim the whole panel (or keep only "Cofnij" and replace the hint with "Pauza: wznów, żeby zapisywać gole").
9. **Desktop games grid with holes (Minor, responsiveness).** Where: 4-desktop/04, 5-wide/04. What: day headings and dividers span only the left column; days with 1 or 3 games leave the right column empty, so it reads as a ragged second list. Fix: make each day a section whose heading spans both columns (`grid-column: 1 / -1`) with that day's games flowing in two columns underneath.
10. **League settings hidden; league actions split across two menus (Minor, IA).** Where: 22, 23, 10, 4-desktop rail. What: invite/switch under the title chevron, settings/language/"who am I" under ≡, no "Ustawienia" in the desktop rail. Fix: one league menu (title chevron) holding invite, switch, settings; keep ≡ for app-level items; add "Ustawienia ligi" to the rail's league section.
