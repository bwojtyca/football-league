## Plan: sign-in and scale

Research: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-19-google-sign-in-research.md`; data step:
`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-19-per-league-data.md`. Order:

1. 2017 migration — **done**.
2. Per-league data — games **done (live)**, tournaments **done (unshipped)**.
3. Anonymous + Google sign-in (`firebase/auth` + `rxfire/auth`, `signInWithPopup`, guests as
   anonymous users linked later with `linkWithPopup`), `users/{uid}` with the player they claim
   (replaces "This is me" on the device for signed-in users); rules: auth required to write.
   **Owner's console steps first:** Firebase console → Authentication → enable Google and
   Anonymous; Settings → Authorized domains → add `bwojtyca.github.io`.
4. League owners/admins (`owner?`, `admins?` on leagues; a one-off script the owner runs gives
   his leagues an owner); settings, delete and restore for admins only; real deletion of a
   league with its games and tournaments by its admins.
5. Members, visibility (`public | link | private`), invite links
   (`leagues/{id}/invites/{code}`); players and the user's leagues loaded by id; league search
   by a name-prefix query.
6. App Check.

Open questions for the owner (in the research file): may anyone play in public leagues or only
members; keep the overall ranking (now "your leagues"); stay on GitHub Pages (popup sign-in) or
move to Firebase Hosting (if the installed iOS app cannot finish the popup); who moderates
ownerless old leagues.
